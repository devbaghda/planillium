## Lessons
- Hunting a dialog button by the name "Close" hits the main window's Close and hides the app to the tray: find dialog buttons by automation id (PrimaryButton / CloseButton), never by "Close".
- The Diary sits at the bottom of Reports: wheel down before clicking its filter buttons (click_input on an off-screen button lands elsewhere); "Show N more" links have a zero rectangle, use Invoke.
- Scratch secrets are memory-only: re-enter the fake AI key after every relaunch, or Ask Claude is hidden.
- A green unit test of a JSON helper does not prove the app path: round-trip a real stub reply through the dialog and read data\mentor-winui.log (D1 passed the suite and fails in the app).
- Sum per-row money cells against the Total row on screen, not only the Total against a reference.
