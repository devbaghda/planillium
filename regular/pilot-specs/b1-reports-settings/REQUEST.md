# Feature request: Reports and Settings batch B1

User (2026-10-23), from live review of both apps. Five small changes.

1. **EUR balance in Reports.** In the Reports page sections Today, This week, This month and This year, show
   two money figures in EUR for that period: the unearned income (the lost/gained-earnings figure the sidebar
   already tracks) and the income from paid work (payable tags). Both in EUR, both per period.
2. **Off-plan nudge wording.** The message "You spent X h (about EUR Y) off-plan this month. Try blocking
   distracting apps during working hours." must say "distracting apps and activities", because distraction
   also comes from activities away from the PC.
3. **Income number boxes (Settings -> Income): thousands formatting, no arrows.** Every money amount box
   (previous salary, each payable tag's monthly net, the new-tag amount) shows the number grouped with a
   thin space as it is typed or left: 2 925, 1 600, 14 000. The little up/down arrows on those boxes are removed.
4. **Previous salary layout.** Previous salary is shown in the same layout the payable tags use: a label
   "<name> -- monthly net (EUR)", the number box, and an "= EUR x,xx/h" readout beside it.
5. **Leaving the add-paid-job line.** While the user is in the "New payable tag" name/amount line, pressing
   Esc or clicking anywhere outside it leaves the line (nothing is added, typed text may stay or clear).

Not to change: how income, earnings or scoring are calculated; saved values stay the same numbers.
