cleanliness). Applies to the saved amount boxes: Previous salary and every payable tag's amount box.

1. Esc in a saved money box discards what was typed, shows the saved amount again, and moves the cursor out of the

3. Esc after typing letters, spaces or "-5" gives the same result as line 1.

clears the line and leaves it). Minus-with-Enter and the 12-digit rule are NOT part of this task; do not change them.
