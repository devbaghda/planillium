# Request -- B1b: money boxes in Settings -> Income (follow-up to B1)

Applies to the money boxes in Settings -> Income (previous salary, each payable tag's monthly net, the new-tag amount).

1. A money box saves when the user leaves it or presses Enter -- not on every keystroke. While typing, nothing is saved.
2. Clicking a Settings -> Income heading must not leave typing focus in the first money box, so stray typing
   cannot change the salary.
3. If the user types a minus sign into a money box, a short hint says amounts cannot be negative. The sign is not
   silently dropped.

No change to how amounts are calculated, displayed (thin-space thousands, no spinners) or stored.
